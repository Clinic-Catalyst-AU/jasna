Behind the shop

Income that keeps coming while Jasna is in America.

The shop runs on three roles. Jasna prescribes. Someone else packs and ships. The shop itself handles the payments, the reminders and the auto-replenish. Jasna's hands are only needed for the part only she can do.

Jasna · prescribes

From anywhere, on her phone. Reads new assessments, opens accounts after a consult, sets each client's routine, adjusts it at reviews. That is all.

A trusted person with a shop login, not a clinic login. They see the order queue, stock on the shelf and delivery questions. They never see routines or skin notes and they cannot prescribe.
