What is actually going on

Not one thing. Everything, a little.

After 50 the skin does not have one problem, it has a collection. Texture, colour, capillaries, lines, pores, a bit of loosening along the jaw and neck. Decades of Queensland sun on top. It arrives all at once, and a single facial or a single machine cannot answer all of it.

The clients I attract are not interested in going the fake way. They still want to look like themselves and they are prepared to put in the work. That is the skin I love treating, because the results are honest. My client who is 80 looks amazing. It is never too late to start looking after it properly.

Think of it like the gym. You do not go once and look fabulous. It is maintenance, consistency, good homecare and protection, and the right treatments in the right order. Skin is the same. That is the plan, and I will not sugarcoat it.

"Nobody has my hands. What you see is what you get. I am black and white, and I will tell you how it is."
How I approach it

Consultation first. Then the plan.

I

I look at all of it together

Texture, colour, lines, laxity, the neck, what you have tried, what you are realistically prepared to do. Two hours if that is what it takes. I want us to get along like two girlfriends and for you to leave understanding your skin.

II

A combination, over time

Laser for texture and colour, skin tightening where that is the ask, skin needling, enzyme therapy and facials in between, LED where it helps, and prescriptive skincare underneath all of it. Which ones and in what order is decided in the room, not on a menu.

III

Treat, review, adjust

A three-hour appointment is normal here. Progress photos, honest reviews, and a long-term maintenance plan so the result lasts.

What a plan looks like

Your Skin Restoration Plan.

Face and neck assessed together, because the neck always tells the truth. A staged journey over months, with facials and LED between the bigger treatments so the skin keeps strengthening.

What a plan includes

  • Face and neck, assessed together
  • The combination of laser, tightening and skin technologies your skin needs, chosen after consultation
  • Facials, enzyme therapy and LED between the bigger treatments
  • Your prescribed homecare, so the work holds between visits
  • Progress reviews and photos at each stage
  • A long-term maintenance plan

Questions I get asked

Straight answers.

Am I too old for this to work?

No. My oldest client is 80 and her skin is beautiful. Skin responds at any age when it is treated properly and looked after between visits.

Will I look done?

Not here. I am into the natural look. The aim is your face, rested and well, and people asking what you are doing differently.

How long is an appointment?

A facial is 90 minutes and can run to two hours. A facial, LED and laser together is about three hours. I am not a quick in and out person. I want you comfortable and understanding what is happening.

Do you do injectables?

Cosmetic medicine is a private conversation after your consultation, and only if it is right for you. Most of what we do here is about the skin itself.
