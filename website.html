Your Skin Restoration Plan.

For skin that needs more than one appointment. Face and neck, a combination of the right laser and skin technologies, a schedule, homecare, LED where it helps, reviews and photos, and a maintenance plan for afterwards.

What a plan includes

  • Face and neck, assessed together
  • The combination of laser and skin technologies your skin needs, chosen after consultation
  • A treatment schedule you can plan your life around
  • Your prescribed homecare, so the work holds between visits
  • LED where appropriate
  • Progress reviews and photos at each stage
  • A long-term maintenance plan

Spreading the cost

Some plans are a considerable investment. If paying for the whole journey upfront is the barrier, external patient finance is available for eligible clients through a third-party provider. You apply directly with them and lending is subject to their eligibility, approval, terms, fees and interest.

About Jasna

Twenty-six years. Still looking at the skin first.

I have been treating skin for 26 years. I take my time. I do not look at a face and put it on whatever machine happens to be popular that month. I look at your skin, your history, what you have tried, your lifestyle and what you are realistically prepared to do, and then I build the treatment around you.

If you have been buying skincare for years and still do not understand what your skin needs, we should talk.

Why can an appointment take hours?

Because assessing, explaining and treating properly takes time, and rushing skin is how people end up spending money twice.

Why do you not use the same treatment on everyone?

Because no two faces arrive with the same history. Different skin, different damage, different tolerance, different plan.

I have tried everything. Why would this be different?

Most people have tried everything one product or one machine at a time. Nobody has sat down and looked at the whole picture. That is the consultation.

Do you run promotions?

No. The plan is built around your skin, not around what is on special this month.
