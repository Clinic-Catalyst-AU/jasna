What is actually going on

What Queensland does to skin.

When someone comes to me at 50 plus who has never had anything done, their skin can look like crocodile skin. Texture, colour, pigmentation, capillaries, redness, lines, pores, scarring. If there was acne, all of it is there. Instead of a clear canvas I get a canvas with all the bits and bobs on it. Then it is restoration.

Scarring and texture are structural. They sit deeper than colour, which is why a peel or a cream never quite got there. They need technologies that work in the deeper layers, staged so the skin can rebuild between rounds, and they need time. Months, not weeks.

The chest and neck are usually worse than the face, because they were never protected and never treated. A face that has been restored above a sun-damaged chest looks wrong. So I assess all of it together and we decide the order.

"You should not have to choose your own laser. That is my job. Twenty-six years of it."
How I approach it

Consultation first. Then the plan.

I

I look at the whole picture first

Face, neck and chest. How deep the damage goes, how the skin tolerates treatment, what you have had done before and why it did not hold. Before and afters from my own clients so you know what a realistic journey looks like.

II

Strengthen, then go deeper

I do not put fragile skin straight onto the strongest laser. Gentle rounds first with enzyme therapy and homecare to build the skin up, then the deeper laser and skin needling for texture and scarring. If I can get the result with less invasion, that is what I do. You are suffering enough.

III

Rounds, rests, reviews

A course, a couple of months off, then the next round if the skin is ready. Photos at each stage. Then we can pinpoint: the neck, around the mouth, around the eyes, instead of doing the whole lot every time.

What a plan looks like

Your Skin Restoration Plan.

This is the plan built for skin that needs more than one appointment. Face, neck and chest, a combination of the right laser and skin technologies, staged in rounds, with homecare, reviews and a maintenance plan for afterwards.

What a plan includes

  • Face, neck and chest, assessed together
  • Laser, skin needling, enzyme therapy and peels, chosen and ordered after consultation
  • Staged rounds with proper rests between them
  • Your prescribed homecare, so the skin keeps rebuilding between visits
  • LED where appropriate
  • Progress reviews and photos at each stage
  • A long-term maintenance plan

Questions I get asked

Straight answers.

How long does restoration take?

Longer than you want. You have been living with this for decades and it will not be undone in one treatment, maybe not in twelve months. A course, a rest, another course. You will see it change along the way, and it will be written down before we start.

Does it hurt? What about downtime?

Laser done properly is non-invasive with very little to no downtime, and I use prescription numbing where it is needed. Deeper work has some recovery and I will tell you exactly what to expect. I will not put you through more than your skin needs.

Can you treat my chest and neck?

Yes, and often they need it more than the face. We assess them together and plan the order so everything matches.

I have had laser before and nothing changed. Why would this be different?

Usually it was one machine, once, with no homecare and no protection. Nobody looked at the whole picture or staged it. That is the consultation, and that is the difference.
